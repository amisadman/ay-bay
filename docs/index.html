Simple, offline-first

AyBay is an open-source application to track your income, expenses, and shared budgets. Thoughtfully built for utility, with your data securely stored on your device.

Download APK View Source Code

Managing money shouldn't require a subscription or giving up your privacy. AyBay is built to be a fast, practical tool for keeping your finances in order, from daily coffee expenses to managing shared apartment rent.

Core Features

Everything you need to manage your money, organized cleanly.

Income & Expense Tracking

Quickly log transactions with categorized tags. Visualize your cash flow through intuitive charts.

Offline-First Architecture

Your personal financial data is stored securely on your device using SQLite. No constant internet connection required.

Collaborative Events

Planning a trip? Create an event, sync via Firebase, invite friends, and split shared expenses seamlessly.

Dedicated Modules

Optional advanced modules allow you to manage small businesses like a shop, gym, or garage right from the app.

Budgeting & Loans

Set category-based monthly budgets and track loans or money owed with partial installment tracking.

Voice & Text Assistant

Optional Walleo integration lets you bring your own Groq API key to log expenses conversationally.

Built on strong principles.

  • Privacy First: Your personal ledger belongs to you. It's stored locally and not sold to advertisers.
  • No Subscriptions: Personal finance tools shouldn't cost you a monthly fee. AyBay is truly free.
  • Simplicity: A flat, fast UI without unnecessary visual clutter. Do what you need to do, quickly.
